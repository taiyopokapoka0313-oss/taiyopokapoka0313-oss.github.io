PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Established: November 20, 2026   Last revised: November 27, 2026

Outumn apps (the "Operator") sets out how information is handled in all iOS apps the Operator provides (FaceUP, the toppa series and others; the "Apps"). Some Apps show ads, some offer in-app purchases and some use the camera, so certain articles apply only to Apps that have the relevant feature.

1. Basic policy

The Apps work offline in principle, and the Operator does not collect or store your personal information on its servers. Study records, training records, settings and similar data are stored only on your device. However, in Apps that show ads, third parties such as Google obtain certain information to deliver ads (Article 5).

2. Information we do not collect

Through the Apps, the Operator does not obtain, collect or store personal information such as your name, address, phone number or email address. The Apps do not require registration or login.

3. Information stored on your device

The Apps store study progress, answer history, scores, exam dates, training records and notification and display settings only on your device. This information is never sent to the Operator.

4. Camera, photos, location and notifications

  • Camera and photos (only Apps that use the camera)   These Apps use the camera to take and record photos of your face and to analyze it, and may ask for access to your photo library to select and save photos. Captured images and face-analysis results are processed and stored only on your device. They are not sent to the Operator's servers and are not used for advertising or provided to any third party.
  • Location   The Apps do not access your device's location, such as GPS. However, in Apps that show ads, Google may estimate your approximate area from information such as your IP address in order to deliver ads (Article 5).
  • Notifications (only Apps with reminders)   Reminders use local notifications generated on your device only. The Apps do not use remote notifications sent from a server. You can change the notification permission at any time in your device's Settings.

Apps that do not use the camera or photos do not access them.

5. Advertising (only Apps that show ads)

Apps that show ads, such as in a free plan, display them through Google LLC's ad service "Google AdMob". As a result, Google and its ad partners may obtain advertising identifiers (IDFA), cookies, device information and usage information to deliver, optimize and measure ads. Their handling follows each company's own policy. Depending on where you live, Google's consent tool (User Messaging Platform) may show a screen asking for your consent about ads. In Apps where a paid plan removes ads, no ads are shown after you purchase it.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information for ads

6. Tracking permission (ATT)

Apps that use the advertising identifier ask for your permission through Apple's "App Tracking Transparency (ATT)". You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. You can still use the Apps if you decline (ads may be less relevant).

7. In-app purchases (only Apps that offer them)

Purchases of paid plans and payments are handled through Apple (the App Store). Payment details such as credit card numbers are managed by Apple, and the Operator neither obtains nor stores them.

8. Information you send us when you contact us

The name, email address and message you send through the contact form (Google Forms) or by email are used only to reply and to handle bugs and requests. We do not provide them to third parties without your consent, except as required by law. Form entries are stored and processed on Google LLC's services.

9. Third-party services used

Depending on the features of each App, the following third-party services may be used. Their handling of information follows their own privacy policies.

  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google User Messaging Platform (ad consent) / Google LLC: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • App Store (in-app purchases) / Apple Inc.: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • Google Forms (contact) / Google LLC: https://policies.google.com/privacy

10. Children's privacy

The Apps are intended for users at or above the age in each App's App Store age rating, and do not knowingly collect children's personal information without parental consent.

11. Retention and deletion

Data on your device is deleted when you delete or reset it within the App or uninstall the App. The Operator holds no data on its servers. Records of inquiries are deleted once they are no longer needed to respond.

12. Requests for disclosure, correction, deletion, etc.

Because the Operator does not store personal information on servers, there is, as a rule, no data that can be the subject of such requests, other than information you sent when contacting us. If you want information from an inquiry disclosed, corrected or deleted, please contact us at the address below. For information related to advertising, please contact Google or the relevant companies.

13. Changes to this policy

The Operator may revise this policy in response to changes in laws or in the service. A revised policy takes effect when it is posted on this page. This policy also applies as-is to any new App the Operator releases.
